The Brokerage

Built on discretion, precision and architectural authority.

Founded in 2008, Aurelia Estates operates as a bespoke real estate advisory representing high-net-worth individuals, institutional trusts, and family offices in the acquisition and disposition of South Florida’s most consequential residential assets.

Our advisory model is deliberately selective: each transaction is governed by strict confidentiality, tailored marketing cinematography, and deep financial structuring to deliver unmatched liquidity for our sellers and exclusive off-market access for our buyers.
