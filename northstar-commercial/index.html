Capital Allocation

Institutional Investment Profiles

We tailor capital deployment across three primary risk-adjusted investment profiles to meet strict internal rate of return (IRR) hurdles.

Core & Single-Tenant Net Lease (NNN)

High-credit investment-grade tenants (Fortune 500, S&P 500) on 10 to 20-year unencumbered NNN leases with zero landlord capital obligations and predictable cash flows.

Target Cap Rates: 5.25% – 6.25% • Target IRR: 8% – 10%

Core-Plus & Light Value-Add

Well-located Class A assets with below-market in-place leases, mark-to-market rollover upside, or minor technology and amenity modernization opportunities.

Target Cap Rates: 6.25% – 7.10% • Target IRR: 12% – 15%

Heavy Value-Add & Adaptive Reuse

Distressed office-to-residential conversions, underutilized industrial infill sites, and shovel-ready land assemblages in high-absorption Sunbelt corridors.

Target Cap Rates: 7.25%+ • Target IRR: 18% – 22%+
